MogTown · Instructor instructions

80-minute class

Seven teams investigate why sales are rising while profits fall. Every team completes all four analytics rounds; TrendTok is the shared news feed, not an eighth team.

1. Organize the class

  1. Divide the class into seven roughly equal groups. Give each group its matching worksheet and private role code. Use one phone or computer and a calculator per group.
  2. Assign an analyst, recorder, spokesperson, and questioner within each group. Combine jobs in smaller groups; share analysis in larger groups.
  3. Students choose their assigned organization and enter its code. Check that everyone can see the briefing and Round 1 before starting.

Seven group assignments

1GlowLabProducts, growth, and profitability
2JawMax LabsProduct performance and customer experience
3MirrorAIRecommendations and customer decisions
4PrimeEra AIHealthy aging and presentation
5CloutHouseCreators and marketing
6Consumer Protection AgencyComplaints and consumer welfare
7Mayor's Economic CouncilThe economy and public policy

2. Run the activity in order

StageMin.Instructor action
Setup5Allocate groups, materials, and jobs. Distribute role codes privately; confirm successful logins.
1 · Descriptive10Ask what happened. Teams record 3 observations, 2 calculations, 1 strange pattern, and 1 unanswered question. Stop premature causal claims.
2 · Diagnostic15Release Round 2. Teams propose a hypothesis, supporting evidence, an alternative explanation, and missing causal evidence. Allow 60 seconds per team and one brief cross-question.
3 · Predictive12Release Round 3. Teams calculate the forecast and state an assumption, key driver, and what could make it wrong.
4 · Prescriptive13Release Round 4. Teams recommend an action with evidence, expected result, trade-off, and 2–3 KPIs. Allow justified custom proposals.
Prepare5Teams prepare a concise recommendation and identify their strongest evidence and main uncertainty.
Hearing & final reveal20Hear every recommendation before releasing the final code. Teams then explain whether their recommendation changes and which new evidence prompted the update.

Truth Rule: Teams may selectively disclose and frame unlocked evidence. They must not invent data or contradict an unlocked metric. Answer direct questions about unlocked metrics truthfully.

Release shared codes in order; students enter them on their own devices. Collect worksheets. Evaluate evidence accuracy, fact versus inference, forecast assumptions, and decisions with trade-offs, KPIs, and evidence-based updates.
